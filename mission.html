Mission

Technology policy is too consequential to be set by adversaries.

Yet the people who build technology and the people who govern it have come to treat each other that way. The distance between them is now written into the rules — whether industries form here or somewhere else, whether America holds its lead, and whether the gains are shared or held by a few. The gap grew out of how the two sides have behaved toward each other, which means it can be closed. Closing it takes time in the same room, and that time has not been spent.

The Open Progress Institute builds those relationships. We put founders, executives, and investors in the same room as the officials and senior staff who write the rules they live by, in invitation-only gatherings held off the record, where people can speak plainly. Relationships come first. Where it helps, we commission research and prepare the briefings that let decision-makers work through hard questions with confidence. We hold the middle, keep the room honest, and carry what gets worked out there to the people whose decisions it should reach.
